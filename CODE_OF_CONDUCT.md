# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Cude.new a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.
- Welcome newcomers and help them contribute safely.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and responsible-disclosure processes.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and community standards.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, repetition, and community safety.

## Reporting

For public conduct concerns, open a GitHub Discussion asking to contact a maintainer privately without including sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and must not be disclosed in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents Cude.new or its community.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
